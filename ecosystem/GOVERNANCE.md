# QAG Ecosystem Governance

## Purpose

This document defines ownership, stewardship, commercialization, and ecosystem growth principles.

---

# Core Principle

The ecosystem is designed for long-term sustainability.

Revenue exists to strengthen the ecosystem.

The ecosystem does not exist solely to generate revenue.

---

# Ecosystem Assets

## Research Assets

- FFI
- DPC
- Publications
- Handouts
- Research Notes

Purpose:
- Knowledge Creation
- Scientific Advancement

---

## Infrastructure Assets

- DPI
- QMS

Purpose:

- Runtime Infrastructure

---

## Validation Assets

- Validation Methodologies

- Trust

- Assessment

---

## Commercial Assets

- Services
- Reports

Purpose:
- Revenue Generation

---

# Open-Core Principle

Open:

- Research
- Publications
- Documentation
- Core Frameworks
- Core Methodologies

Commercial:

- Assessments
- Validation Engagements
- Analytics Services
- Training
- Consulting
- Enterprise Support

---

# Revenue Allocation

Priority 1:
Ecosystem Sustainability

Priority 2:
Platform Development

Priority 3:
Research Expansion

Priority 4:
Founder Compensation

---

# Modularity Principle

Every major subsystem:

- FFI
- DPC
- DPI
- QMS
- QMCTB

must be capable of becoming:

1. A program
2. A product
3. A service
4. A future business unit

while remaining interoperable with the ecosystem.

---

# Spinout Principle

Future initiatives may emerge from ecosystem modules.

Examples:

- DPI Labs
- QMBS Initiative
- QMS Platform
- Training Academy

These initiatives remain aligned with ecosystem objectives.

---

# Ecosystem Success Metric

Success is measured by:

- Scientific Impact
- Ecosystem Activity
- Customer Outcomes
- Revenue Sustainability
- Platform Growth

not by repository size alone.
