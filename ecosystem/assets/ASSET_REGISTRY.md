# QAG Asset Registry

## Purpose

Track every reusable asset generated by the ecosystem.

Every customer engagement must create at least one reusable ecosystem asset.

---

# Asset Types

## Research Assets

- Publications
- Whitepapers
- Technical Notes
- Research Reports

---

## Validation Assets

- Validation Templates
- Assessment Frameworks
- Review Methodologies

---

## Benchmark Assets

- Benchmark Definitions
- Benchmark Datasets
- Benchmark Reports

---

## Analytics Assets

- Analytics Templates
- Analysis Workflows
- Detector Intelligence Reports

---

## Infrastructure Assets

- DPI Components
- QMS Components
- Runtime Modules
- Reusable Tooling

---

# Asset Rule

Every engagement must leave behind:

1. Revenue
2. Knowledge
3. Reusable Asset

No engagement should end with only revenue.
