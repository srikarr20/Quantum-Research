# QAG Revenue Pipeline

## Objective

Convert ecosystem activity into repeatable revenue.

---

# Stage 1

## Discovery

Potential customer becomes aware of QAG.

Sources:

- GitHub
- Publications
- Referrals
- Collaborators
- Existing Network

Output:

Qualified Conversation

---

# Stage 2

## Qualification

Determine:

- Problem
- Urgency
- Budget
- Deliverable Required

Output:

Assessment Opportunity

---

# Stage 3

## Offer Selection

Choose:

- Assessment
- Validation
- Benchmarking
- Analytics
- Consulting

Output:

Defined Scope

---

# Stage 4

## Proposal

Document:

- Scope
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Pricing

Output:

Commercial Proposal

---

# Stage 5

## Engagement

Customer accepts.

Output:

Active Project

---

# Stage 6

## Delivery

Provide:

- Reports
- Analytics
- Validation Results
- Recommendations

Output:

Customer Outcome

---

# Stage 7

## Asset Creation

Convert engagement into:

- Case Study
- Benchmark Artifact
- Reusable Methodology
- Ecosystem Knowledge

Output:

Permanent Ecosystem Asset

---

# Stage 8

## Expansion

Customer becomes:

- Reference
- Repeat Customer
- Collaborator
- Partner

Output:

New Opportunities
