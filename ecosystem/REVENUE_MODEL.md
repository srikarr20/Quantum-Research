# QAG Revenue Model

## Core Principle

The repository is not the product.

The ecosystem generates economic activity.

Revenue is generated from activities enabled by the ecosystem.

## Open Layer

Public and freely available:

- Research Programs
- Publications
- Benchmark Specifications
- Documentation
- GitHub Repositories
- Educational Material

Purpose:
- Adoption
- Credibility
- Community
- Discovery

## Professional Layer

Revenue-generating activities:

### Validation
Independent scientific assessment and review.

### Benchmarking
Performance evaluation and comparison.

### Analytics
Measurement intelligence and detector analytics.

### Training
Knowledge transfer and ecosystem onboarding.

### Consulting
Infrastructure, validation, and measurement architecture support.

## Future Layer

Potential future offerings:

- Hosted Platforms
- Enterprise Integrations
- Certification Programs
- Managed Infrastructure

## Revenue Flywheel

Research
→ Infrastructure
→ Validation
→ Trust
→ Adoption
→ Revenue
→ Ecosystem Growth
