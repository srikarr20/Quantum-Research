# Quantum Research Ecosystem

Welcome to the Quantum Architecture Group (QAG) ecosystem.

## What Is This Repository?

Quantum-Research is an integrated research, infrastructure, validation, and commercialization ecosystem focused on measurement-centric scientific systems.

The ecosystem consists of:

### Research Programs

- Field-First Interpretation (FFI)
- Detector Plane Causality (DPC)

### Infrastructure Programs

- Detector Plane Imaging (DPI)
- Quantum Measurement Stack (QMS)

### Validation Programs

- Quantum Measurement Test Bench (QMCTB)

### Ecosystem Layer

- Products
- Services
- Revenue Models
- Governance

### Business Layer

- Participants
- Partnerships
- Commercial Programs
