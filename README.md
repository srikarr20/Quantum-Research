# Quantum Research Portfolio

Author: Srikar R.

## Overview

This repository consolidates research artifacts, publications, software, simulations, benchmarks, and conceptual frameworks developed across multiple quantum research projects.

The portfolio currently consists of four active research tracks:

### 1. Field-First Interpretation of Wave-Particle Duality

A conceptual framework grounded in Quantum Field Theory that treats quantum fields as fundamental and particles as excitation states of their respective fields.
