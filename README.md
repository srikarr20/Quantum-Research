# Quantum Research Portfolio

## Field-First Research Framework

This repository consolidates a unified body of research focused on quantum measurement, coherence, detector-plane observability, and field-based interpretations of quantum phenomena.

The portfolio is organized around a Field-First perspective in which quantum fields are treated as the fundamental physical substrate, while observable interference, coherence, and measurement outcomes emerge through field evolution and detector-plane interactions.
