# QMVA-03 Executive Summary

Using real superconducting qubit readout
data from the ISTA All-Optical Readout
dataset, QMVA-03 evaluated information
retention at the detector plane.

The audit found that detector-plane
measurements contain substantially more
information than the final binary
classification output.

Detector-plane confidence was shown to
predict classification correctness,
with observed accuracy rising from
65.9% at low confidence to nearly
99% at high confidence.

Filtering low-confidence measurements
reduced classification error by
approximately 27% while retaining
over 92% of detector events.

These results demonstrate that
detector-plane metadata has predictive
and operational value beyond the
information preserved in binary
measurement outcomes.
