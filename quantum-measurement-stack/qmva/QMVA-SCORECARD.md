# QMVA Scorecard Framework

## Purpose

Provide a repeatable methodology for evaluating
information visibility across quantum software stacks.

---

## Categories

### State Retention

Can the platform retain:

- Statevectors
- Density Matrices
- Amplitudes
- Probabilities

Score: /10

---

### Conditional Retention

Can information be retained separately
for distinct measurement branches?

Score: /10

---

### Per-Shot Retention

Can information be preserved
at the individual-shot level?

Score: /10

---

### Projection Transparency

Can users determine:

- what information is selected
- where selection occurs
- why selection occurs

Score: /10

---

### Packaging Transparency

Can users determine:

- how results are transformed
- where API containers are introduced

Score: /10

---

### User Accessibility

Can advanced state information be accessed
without modifying source code?

Score: /10

---

### Documentation Visibility

Are information-retention capabilities
clearly documented?

Score: /10

---

## Composite Metrics

Information Retention Index (IRI)

Measures richness of retained information.

Visibility Accessibility Index (VAI)

Measures accessibility of retained information.

Abstraction Compression Index (ACI)

Measures information reduction across
projection and packaging boundaries.
