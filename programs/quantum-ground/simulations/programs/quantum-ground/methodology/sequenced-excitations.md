# Sequenced Excitations Methodology

## Conceptual Workflow

Localized Excitation
        ↓
Interaction
        ↓
Contextual Imprint
        ↓
Sequenced Preservation
        ↓
Information Emergence
        ↓
Cross-Scale Coherence

## Objective

Track state evolution as a preserved sequence of excitation events
rather than considering only final measurement outcomes.

## Research Questions

1. How does information emerge from excitation histories?
2. Which excitation sequences preserve coherence?
3. How do different histories produce different structures?
4. Can sequence preservation be quantified?

## Initial Metrics

- State history
- Amplitude evolution
- Coherence retention
- Entropy evolution
- Measurement outcome distribution
