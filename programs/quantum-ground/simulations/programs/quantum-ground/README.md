# Quantum Ground Program

## Objective

Investigate how localized excitation events, contextual imprints,
sequenced preservation, and cross-scale coherence contribute to the
emergence of information and observable structure.

## Program Modules

methodology/
    Definitions, workflows, metrics, and research procedures.

simulations/
    Computational models of excitation sequences and information emergence.

benchmarks/
    Validation datasets, reference cases, and reproducibility artifacts.

artifacts/
    Generated outputs, plots, reports, and experimental results.
