# History Metrics

## Excitation History Depth (EHD)

Definition:

Number of excitation events required to reach a final state.

EHD(path) = count(events)

---

## Excitation Path Multiplicity (EPM)

Definition:

Number of distinct excitation histories that converge to an equivalent final state.

EPM(state) = count(distinct paths)

---

## Working Hypothesis

State Information
≠
History Information

A final quantum state may not uniquely determine the excitation
history that produced it.
