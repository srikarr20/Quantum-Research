# Quantum Research Platform

## Platform Purpose

The Quantum Research Platform is the operational framework of QAG.

It connects:

Research
→ Infrastructure
→ Validation
→ Analytics
→ Commercial Services

within a single ecosystem.

## Platform Components

### Research Programs
- Field-First Interpretation (FFI)
- Detector Plane Causality (DPC)

### Infrastructure Programs
- Detector Plane Imaging (DPI)
- Quantum Measurement Stack (QMS)

### Validation Programs
- Quantum Measurement Test Bench (QMCTB)

## Platform Outputs

- Publications
- Benchmarks
- Validation Reports
- Analytics Reports
- Training Materials
- Infrastructure Components

## Platform Stakeholders

- Researchers
- Academic Labs
- National Labs
- Scientific Software Teams
- Deep-Tech Startups
- Government Programs

## Platform Objective

Create a self-sustaining ecosystem where scientific activity generates infrastructure, infrastructure generates validation, validation generates trust, and trust generates revenue.
