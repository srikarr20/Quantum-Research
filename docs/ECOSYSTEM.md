# Quantum Architecture Group (QAG)

## What We Are

QAG is a measurement-centric research ecosystem.

The ecosystem integrates:

- Field-First Interpretation (FFI)
- Detector Plane Causality (DPC)
- Detector Plane Imaging (DPI)
- Quantum Measurement Stack (QMS)
- Quantum Measurement Test Bench (QMCTB)

into a unified architecture for scientific research, validation, benchmarking, analytics, and infrastructure development.

## Mission

To build an open, modular ecosystem that enables scientific validation, measurement intelligence, detector analytics, benchmarking, and research infrastructure.

## Core Principle

The ecosystem is greater than any individual subsystem.
